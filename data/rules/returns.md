# Returns

## Return window
Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 365 days of purchase for a full refund.

## Assembled products
Assembled products can be returned within 180 days if they are clean and undamaged.

## What you need
Bring the order number. Without an order number the store offers a store credit instead of a refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Cut-to-size items and items sold "as-is" cannot be returned.
