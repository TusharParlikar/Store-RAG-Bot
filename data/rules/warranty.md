# Warranty

## How long the warranty lasts
Each product's warranty length is listed in its `warranty_months` column. The warranty starts on the purchase date.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal home use.

## What the warranty does not cover
Normal wear and tear, cuts, scratches, damage from accidents, misuse, wrong assembly, and commercial use of home products are not covered.

## How to make a claim
Bring or send the order number and a photo of the problem to the store. The store repairs or replaces the item.
